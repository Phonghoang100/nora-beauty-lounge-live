The Nora Standard

What does a lash lounge do differently?

Most lash bars are built for throughput. Nora is built for the ninety minutes you spend on the bed, and for how your lashes look on the mornings after.

01

Designed for you

Every set begins with a consultation. Your artist maps your eye shape, lash health and daily routine before a single extension is placed.

02

A private, quiet room

One artist, one client, one calm room. No overlapping conversations and no rushing to the next chair.

03

Honest guidance

If a fill won't look its best, we'll tell you a new set is the better choice. Careful aftercare keeps most clients comfortably ahead of that line.
